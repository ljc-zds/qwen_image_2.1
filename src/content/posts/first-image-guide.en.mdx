## Start with one clear idea

Choose a subject, a setting and a visual style. A focused prompt is easier to refine than a long list of competing instructions.

> An orange astronaut cat holding a glowing star, pale yellow background, soft pastel illustration, full body, no text.

## Choose the composition

Open [Image generator](/image-generator). Choose a square format for a profile image or sticker, a wide format for a banner, or a portrait format for a poster. Describe framing and lighting when those details matter.

## Generate and refine

Sign in to a verified account. The free welcome allowance is one image, once per verified account, subject to daily availability. Each successful image uses one credit; failed generations return the reserved credit.

If the result needs a focused change, use [Image editor](/image-editor). Upload your reference and specify both what should stay and what should change.

## Find your saved work

New successful images are saved privately to your account. Open [My creations](/my-creations) to view the image, read its prompt or download the PNG. Images created before saving was enabled cannot be recovered from the library.
