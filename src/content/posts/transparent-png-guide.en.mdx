## Describe an isolated subject

Open [Transparent PNG](/transparent-png). Describe one subject, its style and its outline. Ask for an isolated subject with a transparent background and avoid requesting a detailed scene behind it.

> A small orange astronaut cat sticker, full body, clean silhouette, transparent background, no text.

## Transparency is different from a white background

A PNG can contain a solid white background. A transparent PNG includes pixels with transparency. The studio checks the returned image for transparent pixels; a failed transparency check returns the reserved credit.

A generated result can still contain unwanted background elements. Inspect the image on both light and dark backgrounds before placing it in a design.

## Download and reuse

Download the PNG from the result or from [My creations](/my-creations). Keep the PNG format when you need transparency: converting to JPEG removes it.

Use a larger subject with a clear silhouette for small stickers. Avoid tiny text or fine background details, and check the edges at the final display size.
